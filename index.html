About

I am an AI & Linguistics researcher and engineer with interests at the intersection of natural language processing, multimodal reasoning, and educational AI. I'm particularly drawn to questions about how language models represent discourse structure, and how vision-language models interact with quasi-symbolic and directional representations in images.

Professionally, I work at Mercer Mettl (Marsh & McLennan Companies), an AI-based psychometric assessments platform, where I build real-world assessment and proctoring systems. I have also spent the last two years collaborating with Dr. Vivek Gupta and the Cognitive Computation Group at UPenn (now ASU) on benchmark development for multimodal reasoning.

I hold a B.Tech. in Computer Science from Manipal University Jaipur (CGPA 9.35/10, Dean's List). I serve as a peer reviewer for ACL ARR and IEEE Transactions on Audio, Speech & Language Processing.
